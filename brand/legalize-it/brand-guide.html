The mark

The L is the checkmark

Every filing ends the same way: an approval. The mark takes the L of Legalize, turns it 45 degrees so it reads as a check, and sets it inside a seal ring like the stamp on a certificate of incorporation.

An LShort stem, long foot. Same weight as the wordmark.
Turned 45°The corner becomes the vertex of a check.
SealedA Charter Green disc with a Seal Gold ring. Approved, stamped, done.

The wordmark is set in lowercase Bricolage Grotesque Bold. The tittle on the i of “it” is replaced with a gold diamond, the same shape used for section markers across the product.

Clear space & size

Give the seal room

Keep clear space equal to a quarter of the mark’s height on every side. Nothing else (text, edges, other logos) enters that zone.

Clear space = ¼ × mark heightAt 48px tall, that is 12px all round.
Minimum sizesBelow these, switch to the mark alone.
Primary32px tall on screen · 12mm in print
Stacked64px tall on screen · 22mm in print
Mark20px on screen · 6mm in print

Voice

Sound like a sharp lawyer who explains things

Plain English, short sentences, and real numbers. Name the agency, the fee and the timeline. Never scare people into buying, and never promise an outcome the regulator controls.

SayCAC usually approves a business name in 2 to 5 working days. We’ll message you the moment it’s through.
Don’t sayGet registered in 24 hours, guaranteed! Avoid costly penalties before it’s too late!
SayYour trademark protects the name “Adaeze Foods” for food products in Class 30. It doesn’t cover clothing.
Don’t sayPursuant to the provisions of the Trade Marks Act, the applicant shall be entitled to exclusive use thereof.
