01 Logo

The L is the checkmark

The mark takes the L from Legalize and turns it 45 degrees so it reads as a tick, the moment a filing is approved. It sits inside a ring like the seal on a certificate of incorporation.

An LShort stem, long foot
Turned 45°The corner becomes the tick
SealedGreen disc, gold ring

The wordmark is lowercase Libre Franklin ExtraBold with tightened spacing. The dot of the i is coloured gold to echo the seal ring. Always use the supplied files; never retype the name.

05 Typography

Libre Franklin

One family for everything, from the wordmark to the fine print. It is based on Franklin Gothic, a typeface with a hundred years of use in newspapers and public signage, so it reads as established rather than trendy. Free on Google Fonts, with the naira sign ₦.

08 Voice

How we write

Plain English. Name the agency, the fee and the timeline. Don’t frighten people into buying, and never promise an outcome only a regulator controls.

Write thisNot this
CAC usually approves a business name in 2 to 5 working days. We’ll message you when it’s through.Get registered in 24 hours, guaranteed!
Your trademark covers “Adaeze Foods” for food products in Class 30. It doesn’t cover clothing.The applicant shall be entitled to exclusive use thereof pursuant to the Act.
Annual returns are due. Filing late adds a penalty, so we’ve started yours.Avoid costly penalties before it’s too late!
